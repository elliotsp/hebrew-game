"Get on your bike and go"

A Season 2 promo: a jogger, a man on a motorcycle and an unmarked metal gate. It opens as a street confrontation ("Hey clown, can't you see how you're riding?"), then the gate opens and the mood flips to teammates teasing each other. yes titles it דיר בלאק אתם מאחרים! — דיר בלאק is Arabic street slang Israelis use all the time for "watch it / don't you dare". Listen for יא ליצן, יאללה… סע מפה, and שמעתי עליך כבר.
