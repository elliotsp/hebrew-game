"Answer already!"

A one-minute fight in a dark kitchen. yes titles it דורון וגלי בפיצוץ (Doron and Gali blow up). Someone suspects an affair with a friend, demands an answer, and gets a blunt one. This is how Israelis actually argue: impatient, sarcastic, short. Listen for נו, then כבר, then על מה את מדברת?
Official clip from yes tv (יס), the Israeli broadcaster that made Fauda · פאודה: דורון וגלי בפיצוץ · 1:03 · Open on YouTube
How to use this: watch once with no help. Then go through the phrases and tap ▶ in clip to jump to that line. Watch again at the end. The phrase cards follow the Hebrew subtitles that yes burned into the clip; jump times were read off the video frames.
Heads-up on language: two lines in this clip use crude sexual slang (the verbs מזדיין / לזיין). You will hear them in Israel — לזיין also gets used as "to screw someone over" — so it's worth recognizing them, but they're vulgar and not taught as cards here.
